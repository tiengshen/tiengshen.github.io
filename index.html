What I plan with you我与您一起规划的事项

One protects what you earn. The other protects what you leave.一份守护您的收入，一份守护您留下的资产。

Income protection收入保障

If illness, disability or death stops your income, the bills do not stop. The aim is a plan that keeps your family's lifestyle, loans and children's education on track.当疾病、残疾或身故令收入中断，开销并不会停。目标是让家人的生活、贷款和子女教育不受影响。

  • Life cover人寿保障 that replaces income and clears loans.替代收入并清还贷款。
  • Critical illness cover严重疾病保障 for treatment costs and months away from work.应对治疗费用与停工期间的开销。
  • Disability cover残疾保障 when you can no longer earn as before.当您无法像以前一样赚取收入。
  • Medical cover医药保障 so a hospital bill does not drain your savings.避免一笔医药费耗尽积蓄。

Asset and legacy planning资产与传承规划

Passing on wealth takes more than leaving it behind. I help you set aside a defined sum, name who receives it, and give your family cash when they need it.传承不只是把财富留下。我协助您预留一笔明确的资金、指定受益人，并让家人在需要时有现金可用。

  • Defined legacy sums明确的传承金额 for spouse, children or parents.留给配偶、子女或父母。
  • Cash for debts and final expenses债务与身后费用的现金 so assets need not be sold in a hurry.不必仓促变卖资产。
  • Nominations reviewed检视受益人提名 so proceeds go where you intend.让保障金流向您希望的人。
  • Business continuity企业传承与延续 funding for key-person and partner risk.应对关键人物与合伙人风险。

Wills and trusts are covered below. For hibah or complex family situations, a lawyer's advice is best, and I am happy to work alongside yours.遗嘱与信托见下方。赠与（Hibah）或较复杂的家庭情况，建议咨询律师，我乐意与您的律师配合。

Will writing撰写遗嘱

Without a will, your wishes may not be followed. I help you prepare one so your family knows what you intended.没有遗嘱，您的心愿未必能被执行。我协助您准备遗嘱，让家人清楚您的意愿。

  • Name who receives what指定谁得到什么 across your assets.涵盖您的各项资产。
  • Appoint an executor委任遗嘱执行人 to carry out your wishes.负责执行您的心愿。
  • Name a guardian指定监护人 for young children.照顾年幼的孩子。
  • Review it定期检视 when your family or assets change.家庭或资产有变动时更新。

Wills are prepared through KOHA. KOHA's own terms and fees apply.遗嘱通过 KOHA 办理，适用 KOHA 本身的条款与收费。

Trust set-up设立信托

A trust lets you decide when and how your family receives assets, with a professional trustee to administer them.信托让您决定家人何时、以何种方式获得资产，并由专业受托人负责管理。

  • Control timing掌握时间 of when beneficiaries receive funds.决定受益人何时获得资金。
  • Provide for dependants照顾受养人 who cannot yet manage money themselves.例如尚不能自行理财的孩子。
  • Professional trustee专业受托人 to administer the assets.负责管理资产。

Trusts are set up through trust partners, including Affin Hwang. Each provider's own terms and fees apply.信托通过合作的信托机构办理，包括 Affin Hwang，适用各机构本身的条款与收费。

Common questions常见问题

Before we speak.面谈之前。

How much cover do I need?我需要多少保额？

It depends on your income, loans, dependants and what you already own. The calculator above gives a rough starting point. We refine it together in a conversation.取决于您的收入、贷款、受养人，以及您已有的资产。上面的计算器可作粗略起点，我们面谈时再一起细化。

Is legacy planning only for wealthy families?传承规划只适合富裕家庭吗？

No. Any family with a home, loans or dependants has something to pass on. Starting with a clear sum and clear nominations is often the most useful first step.不是。只要有房子、贷款或受养人，就有需要传承的事。从明确的金额与受益人提名开始，往往是最实用的第一步。

Does insurance replace a will?保险可以取代遗嘱吗？

No. Insurance can pay a defined sum to the people you name, but it does not cover your other assets. A will covers the rest, and I can arrange one for you through KOHA. For hibah or complex family situations, speak to a lawyer.不可以。保险可以把一笔明确的金额交给您指定的人，但不涵盖您的其他资产。其余部分由遗嘱处理，我可以通过 KOHA 为您安排。赠与（Hibah）或较复杂的家庭情况，请咨询律师。

What should I prepare for the first conversation?第一次面谈需要准备什么？

A rough idea of your income, loans and any policies you already hold. If you have policy documents, bring them. If not, we can start without them.大概的收入、贷款，以及您现有的保单资料。有保单文件可以带来，没有也可以先开始。

Who prepares the will and the trust?遗嘱和信托由谁办理？

Your will is prepared through KOHA, and your trust is set up through trust partners such as Affin Hwang. I guide you through the process and coordinate with them. These services are separate from Great Eastern insurance plans, and each provider's own terms and fees apply.遗嘱通过 KOHA 办理，信托则通过 Affin Hwang 等合作信托机构设立。我会全程协助并与他们沟通。这些服务与大东方保险计划各自独立，适用各机构本身的条款与收费。
